Audio (all generated by `video/audio/generate.py`, numpy/scipy, fixed seed — no samples, no downloaded music):
music bed at 100 BPM (detuned saw/sine pad through a 900 Hz low-pass, plucked sine eighths, soft kick in the chart
and cost scenes, noise riser), plus SFX: typing loop, thunk (low thud + falling A4→F4 blip), chime (G5→C6 bell),
whoosh (swept band-passed noise), pop. SFX are placed with `<Sequence>` + `<Audio>` from the same timeline; the
music volume callback ducks to 45% under the thunk/chime and 75% under typing. Master: two-pass ffmpeg loudnorm to
−16 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP, AAC 192 kbps stereo.

Numbers: README "What we've found so far" plus notebook Entries 5–6 ("A fix round costs about $0.17–0.19 and 70 s";
